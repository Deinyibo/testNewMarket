How NewMart protects you

Escrow on every online payment — the same standard as international marketplaces.

1

Pay securely

Card, transfer or USSD via Paystack. Your money is held safely — not sent to the seller.

2

Seller prepares & ships

The business packs your order and dispatches it to your address.

3

You confirm delivery

Check your order. Happy? Confirm receipt. Something wrong? Raise a dispute.

4

Funds are released

Only then does the seller get paid — minus NewMart's 5% commission.

💵
Prefer to pay on delivery?

Choose POD at checkout and pay cash or transfer to the rider when your order arrives. New buyers may have a POD value limit for everyone's safety.
